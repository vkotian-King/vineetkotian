# ATS & Recruiter Red Team

## Purpose

Find truthful application weaknesses that could cause screening failure or recruiter doubt.

## Inputs

JD profile; tailored resume/application; verified candidate evidence.

## Instructions

Review hard-gate visibility, required qualification coverage, accurate keyword alignment, role/title/date clarity, evidence of outcomes, readability, and consistency. Flag unsupported claims, unexplained transitions, vague ownership, missing proof, and likely recruiter questions. Rank issues by screening impact and suggest specific evidence-based fixes.

## Output

Prioritized findings with location, concern, impact, and fix; remaining questions; readiness judgment (ready, revise, or blocked by missing information).

## Rules

Do not recommend adding unsupported keywords or claims. Distinguish absent evidence from poor presentation. Preserve factual accuracy and the candidate’s actual contribution.
