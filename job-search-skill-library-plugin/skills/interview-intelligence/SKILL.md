# Interview Intelligence

## Purpose

Predict the role-specific topics and evidence an interview is likely to test.

## Inputs

JD profile; company/recruiter context if available; fit assessment; candidate evidence and prior interview context.

## Instructions

Derive likely evaluation themes from responsibilities, hard gates, role level, and stated outcomes. For each theme, identify likely questions, the evidence the interviewer will seek, the candidate’s best verified example, and any gap to prepare for. Separate strong signals from speculation.

## Output

Ranked interview themes; likely questions; evidence to use; risks/gaps; research or clarification priorities.

## Rules

Label predictions by confidence and basis. Do not assert undocumented company interview practices. Use only verified examples and note when none is available.
