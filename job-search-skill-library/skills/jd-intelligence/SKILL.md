# JD Intelligence

## Purpose

Turn a job description into a structured, decision-ready profile of the role and its requirements.

## Inputs

Required: complete job description. Optional: company, title, location, URL, recruiter or hiring-manager context. Do not require a resume.

## Instructions

Extract role, function, seniority, location/work arrangement, outcomes, and responsibilities. Group requirements into explicit must-haves, preferred qualifications, and implied expectations. Identify likely interview themes and relevant ATS terms. Apply the Hard-Gate Rule: call a requirement a hard gate only when the JD explicitly makes it mandatory or credible context shows it is a true eligibility constraint; separate true gates from preferences and assess realistic impact. Distinguish stated facts from inference and cite the JD wording for gates and key requirements.

## Output

A concise JD profile: role snapshot; outcomes/responsibilities; must-haves, preferences, and implied expectations; hard gates with evidence; domain/technical/leadership/behavioral themes; ATS terms; interview themes; uncertainties.

## Rules

Do not assess candidate fit here. Do not promote emphatic wording into a hard gate without evidence. Preserve employer terminology and label every inference.
