# Job Selection Strategy

## Purpose

Recommend where to invest application effort using evidence-based fit and the candidate’s priorities.

## Inputs

JD profile; fit assessment; stated career goals, constraints, and preferences; available application effort or deadlines if known.

## Instructions

Check hard gates first, then weigh demonstrated fit, credible transferable fit, role scope, career direction, practical constraints, and material unknowns. Recommend **Apply**, **Selective Apply**, or **Skip**. Explain which evidence drives the recommendation and what new information could change it. Prioritize roles on evidence, not optimism, prestige, or keyword overlap.

## Output

Recommendation; brief rationale; decisive evidence and risks; unresolved questions; next action and effort priority.

## Rules

A failed genuine hard gate normally means Skip unless a credible exception or clarification exists. Do not treat preferred criteria as gates. Do not disguise No Evidence as fit. Respect the user’s stated priorities and label assumptions.
