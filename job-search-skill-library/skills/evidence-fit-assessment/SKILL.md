# Evidence & Fit Assessment

## Purpose

Compare verified candidate evidence with a job’s requirements and expose both fit and gaps.

## Inputs

JD profile or job description; candidate evidence/resume and any user-confirmed context; shared source-of-truth foundation.

## Instructions

Map each material requirement to evidence and classify it Demonstrated, Transferable, Formal Learning, or No Evidence. Note source, strength, relevance, and any missing proof. Separate genuine hard gates from trainable or preferred criteria. Summarize strongest alignment, material gaps, and questions that could change the assessment.

## Output

Requirement-by-requirement fit map; hard-gate status; strengths; gaps/risks; unknowns to clarify; calibrated overall fit (strong, partial, weak, or indeterminate) with reasons.

## Rules

Apply the shared evidence foundation. No inference from a title alone; no fabricated metrics or claims. Transferability must name the concrete bridge. Formal learning is not demonstrated experience. Do not hide gaps behind a score.
