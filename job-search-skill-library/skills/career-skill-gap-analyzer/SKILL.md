# Career Skill-Gap Analyzer

## Purpose

Identify recurring capability gaps that matter to the candidate’s chosen career direction.

## Inputs

Multiple JD profiles or target-role pattern; fit assessments; source-of-truth evidence; user’s career goals and learning constraints.

## Instructions

Aggregate repeated requirements and compare them with evidence classifications. Separate genuine recurring gaps from one-off preferences and weakly supported assumptions. Rank gaps by role relevance, frequency, and likely impact. Suggest practical learning or experience-building actions and evidence that would demonstrate progress.

## Output

Prioritized gap list; supporting role/evidence pattern; impact; recommended development actions; proof-of-progress signals; items to deprioritize.

## Rules

Do not infer a gap from one JD unless it is a hard gate for a chosen target. Do not confuse No Evidence with inability. Formal learning may close knowledge gaps but does not itself demonstrate applied experience. Respect the candidate’s stated direction and constraints.
